scope_cache/demo.py is a new compatible adapter informed by the public V2 schema,
demo_parser/extract.py and scripts/training_data/label_builder.py. It does not
include or load CS-NET checkpoints. Survival is a retrospective observed label,
not a prediction or a complete measure of player quality. Unknown winners and
missing event lists remain unknown. The upstream Mirage example was used only
to validate the importer; its observations were not mixed with Cache data.
